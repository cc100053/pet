2Merging 1a + 1c — with the photo message treated as untouchable

The rule I'm now designing to: the photo message is the content, not a texture. So the photo zone keeps your widget's ~1.72 ratio, a clean 2.5px border, and nothing overlaps it — no pet sprite crossing the edge, no rarity plate, no unread badge. Everything collectible moves to the mount (the rarity-tinted outer rim) and the mat (the band below the photo), which is also where the caption and sender live. Unread hangs off the mount's corner, outside both. Result: 1c's loot feel, 1a's polaroid warmth, 1a's data — and the photo is bigger than in either.

2aMount + mat — rarity lives in the 5px mount and a printed plate on the mat; the pet sits in a punched circle on the mat, half-out of the frame but never on the photo. Caption gets its own line.
