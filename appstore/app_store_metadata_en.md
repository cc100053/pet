### Description
[ PetTomo: The Shared Virtual Pet Game for Couples and Friends ]

Looking for a cute way to stay connected with your favorite person? PetTomo is the shared virtual pet app designed for couples, best friends, and family. Co-parent an adorable digital companion and turn your daily moments into a rewarding journey of growth and bonding.

Whether you are in a long-distance relationship (LDR) or just want a new way to share your day, PetTomo brings you closer through the simple act of raising a pet together.

---

[[ CO-RAISE WITH LOVE ]]
Invite your partner or best friend to your shared room. Together, you will care for a unique virtual pet that grows based on your interactions. It is the perfect, low-stress way to build a daily ritual together.

[[ PHOTO-TO-FEED SYSTEM ]]
Your daily photos are the fuel! Take a picture of your lunch, your commute, or a sunset and send it to your pet. Watch as your pet "eats" your memories and grows happier with every share. It creates a beautiful, living photo diary of your relationship.

[[ DECORATE YOUR SHARED HOME ]]
Earn rewards to buy furniture, cute accessories, and beautiful backgrounds. Now with -- Adjustable Furniture Sizes -- you can customize your pet's room to perfectly reflect your shared style and creativity.

[[ STAY CONNECTED VIA CHAT ]]
Our built-in room chat lets you react to each other's photos and stay in touch while looking after your pet. Every notification is a gentle reminder that someone special is thinking of you.

[[ COLLECT PRECIOUS MEMORIES ]]
Look back through your pet's photo diary to relive all the small moments that make your bond special. See how much your pet—and your relationship—has grown over time.

- COUPLES: A fun, interactive "couple app" to stay connected throughout the day.

- BEST FRIENDS: Create a digital mascot for your friendship and decorate together.
- VIRTUAL PET LOVERS: A modern, social twist on the classic virtual pet experience.
