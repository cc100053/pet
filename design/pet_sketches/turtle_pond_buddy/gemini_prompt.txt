Create a new, original turtle character called Little Pond Buddy for a cozy mobile pet game.

Character direction:
- A cute upright pond turtle standing on two short, broad feet, with small rounded hands.
- A compact olive-green shell attached to its back, visible beside the body. Keep the shell modest in size so it does not dominate the character.
- A distinctive leaf-shaped tail growing naturally from the lower body behind the shell. The tail itself has a soft leaf-like silhouette: it is part of the turtle's body, not a plant or an attached leaf. Make it visible and easy to recognise.
- Sage-green skin, a warm cream belly, subtle peach cheeks, and expressive warm-brown eyes.
- Gentle, curious, slightly awkward personality; a little friend who takes life slowly.

Art direction:
- Flat 2D hand-painted digital gouache illustration, soft brush texture, subtle grain and slightly uneven edges.
- Muted, warm, earthy colours; simple colour areas with only one or two shading tones.
- Soft outlines in darker versions of the surrounding colours, never pure black.
- Bold, simple shapes and sparse shell markings, readable at small mobile-game size.
- No glossy 3D rendering, plastic or clay appearance, photorealism, or white sticker border.

Invent your own charming proportions, facial expression, shell pattern and natural standing pose. A slight three-quarter view should show both the face and shell. Keep the design simple enough to animate, with every limb and the tail visibly connected to the body.

Output one complete full-body character, centred in a square image with a comfortable margin. Pure flat white #FFFFFF background. No scenery, floor, cast shadow, text, clothing or props. The character's coloured edges touch the background directly, with no white outline or halo.
