Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I invite a friend to my room?
A: Go to your Room Settings, click on "Invite Friend," and share the unique room code with them. They can enter this code on their home screen to join.
Q: My pet isn't growing?
A: Pets grow based on daily care. Make sure to feed your pet by taking photos of food or objects, and clean up after them regularly!
Q: How do I delete my account?
A: You can delete your account, including all your data and photos, by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account within the app.
Q: Why isn't the AI recognizing my photo?
A: Ensure the object is clearly visible and well-lit. Sometimes complex backgrounds can confuse the pet. Try moving closer to the object!
